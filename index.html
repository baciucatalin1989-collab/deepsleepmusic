Our Story

Crafted for the Liminal Hours

DeepSleep was born from a simple obsession: the belief that sound can reshape consciousness. Not as distraction, but as architecture — building rooms in the mind where rest feels inevitable.

Every composition is built from field recordings, synthesized drones, and carefully tuned frequencies layered until the boundary between music and environment completely dissolves.

We create for insomniacs, overthinkers, meditators, and anyone who has ever stared at the ceiling at 3am wondering where the quiet went.
